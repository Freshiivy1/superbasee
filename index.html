You do. It’s published under your own Apple and Google developer accounts. Apple charges A$149 a year and Google a one time US$25, and we help you set up both.

Not in the build price. We can register a domain for you or connect one you already own, and we show you the costs first.

Every payment goes through Stripe’s secure checkout. We never see your card details.
